Phone → OneDrive Safe Mover

Copies files to OneDrive, checks every copy, and never deletes anything from your phone.

One-time setup (Microsoft app ID)

To reach your OneDrive, this page needs a free "app registration" from Microsoft. You do this once.

  1. Open https://entra.microsoft.com → App registrations → New registration.
  2. Name: Phone OneDrive Mover. Account type: Accounts in any organizational directory and personal Microsoft accounts.
  3. Redirect URI: choose platform Single-page application (SPA) and paste exactly:
  4. Click Register. Copy the Application (client) ID and paste it below.

3Choose files on your phone

In the picker, tap ☰ or "Browse" to reach Internal storage (DCIM, Download, WhatsApp, etc.). Your originals stay where they are.

4Upload and verify

Each run creates a new folder named with the date and time (e.g. 2026-11-26_21-31-05) inside the folder from step 2. "Retry failed files" puts files into that same folder.

Keep this page open and the screen on until it finishes. Each file is checked by size and by Microsoft's file fingerprint (quickXorHash) after it is uploaded.

5Free up space on the phone (you do this)

This page cannot and will not delete anything from your phone. Delete originals yourself, and only the ones marked ✔ Verified. Files marked failed are still safe on your phone; just retry them.
